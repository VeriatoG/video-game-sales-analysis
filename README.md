Video Game Sales Analysis

Market Trends, Platform Performance & Regional Preferences

Author: Gabriela Veriato
Project Type: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Tools: Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | SciPy | Jupyter Notebook

---

Project Overview

This project explores historical video game sales data to identify market trends, evaluate platform performance, understand regional consumer preferences, and investigate the relationship between game ratings and sales.

The analysis aims to identify relevant market patterns and support business decisions regarding platform performance, game genres, and regional opportunities.

Business Questions

- How has the video game industry evolved over time?
- Which gaming platforms generated the highest sales?
- What is the average lifecycle of a gaming platform?
- How do critic and user reviews relate to game sales?
- Which genres and platforms perform best across different regions?
- Are there statistically significant differences in user ratings between platforms and genres?

Tools & Technologies

- Python: Data analysis and statistical computing.
- Pandas: Data cleaning, manipulation and aggregation.
- NumPy: Numerical operations.
- Matplotlib & Seaborn: Data visualization.
- SciPy: Statistical hypothesis testing.
- Jupyter Notebook: Interactive analysis environment.

Project Workflow

1. Data Preparation: Cleaning, formatting, handling missing values and creating global sales metrics.
2. Exploratory Data Analysis: Analysis of annual releases, sales trends and platform lifecycles.
3. Market Analysis: Evaluation of platform performance and genre profitability.
4. Correlation Analysis: Investigation of the relationship between critic/user scores and sales.
5. Regional Analysis: Comparison of consumer preferences in North America, Europe and Japan.
6. Hypothesis Testing: Statistical comparison of user ratings across platforms and genres.

Key Findings

- Gaming platforms generally have a lifecycle of approximately 7–10 years.
- PS4 and Xbox One showed strong market potential for 2017.
- Critic scores demonstrated a moderate positive correlation with sales, while user scores showed little correlation.
- Action and Shooter genres presented strong commercial performance.
- Regional preferences differ significantly, particularly in Japan, where Role-Playing games have a stronger presence.
- Statistical testing identified differences in user ratings between Action and Sports genres, while no statistically significant difference was found between Xbox One and PC ratings.
